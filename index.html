Research & Capstone

Capstone Project

Association Between School Bullying, Cyberbullying & Adolescent Mental Health

Population-based epidemiologic study using CDC YRBSS 2021 data (N = 15,545). Applied multivariable logistic regression in SAS, identifying an adjusted OR of ~15.8 between physical and cyberbullying, and ~2.5× elevated risk in sexual minority youth.

Findings directly inform evidence-based interventions for adolescent mental health risk identification and prevention - closely aligned with Mount Sinai BCRP and FLASH health equity intervention models.
